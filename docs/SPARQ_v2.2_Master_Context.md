## Metrics
### 1. Accuracy
This metric assesses the correctness of the AI model's predictions compared to the ground truth. It is crucial for understanding how well the model performs its intended tasks.

### 2. Precision
Precision measures the proportion of true positive predictions relative to the total predicted positives. This metric is essential in evaluating the reliability of the model in specific contexts.

### 3. Recall
Recall, or sensitivity, quantifies the model’s ability to identify all relevant instances in the dataset. It is a critical metric for applications where missing a positive instance is costly.

### 4. F1 Score
The F1 Score is the harmonic mean of precision and recall, providing a balanced measure for models where both false positives and false negatives are important to minimize.

### 5. AUC-ROC
The Area Under the Receiver Operating Characteristic Curve indicates the model’s performance across all classification thresholds, providing insight into its ability to discriminate between positive and negative classes.

### 6. Latency
Latency measures the time taken by the AI model to produce an output after receiving an input. This metric is particularly significant in real-time applications where response time is critical.

### 7. Resource Utilization
Resource utilization assesses the computational resources, such as CPU and memory usage, consumed by the AI model during inference. This metric is vital for understanding the efficiency and scalability of the solution.
