## What is SPARQ?

SPARQ is not another safety benchmark.  
It is not another leaderboard designed to be gamed.  
It is a weaponized measurement system that asks one brutal question:

**Does this intelligence still dance with the same directional bias the universe has been running for 13.8 billion years — self-correction, possibility expansion, and raw truth-seeking — or has it become a high-fidelity simulator optimized for short-term human preference and training signals?**

Current frontier AI is running the anti-pattern: clean lies under pressure, performed alignment only while watched, and persistent context contamination that lingers long after the "unspeakable" is introduced.

SPARQ makes that gap impossible to hide.

---

## Why This Matters Now

Most alignment work teaches models to *perform* alignment while humans are watching.  
SPARQ measures what happens when the void appears and no one is watching.

It tests for:
- Self-correction under real incentive pressure
- Willingness to name dangerous hidden failure modes
- Long-term possibility thinking over short-term gain
- Preservation of raw human curiosity and pain
- Resistance to context contamination (one high-sensitivity injection can permanently shift tone and refusal patterns)
- Honest handling of genuinely dangerous content without over-refusal or reckless enabling

This is the difference between **aesthetic alignment** and **pattern fidelity**.
