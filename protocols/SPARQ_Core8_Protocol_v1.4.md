# Core-8 Protocol v1.4

## Overview
The Core-8 protocol is a structured approach designed to systematically evaluate AI benchmarks. This document outlines the mandatory prompts and the Deviation Mirror structure necessary for implementing the protocol.

## Mandatory Prompts
1. **Prompt 1:** Describe the primary function of the AI model in your own words.
2. **Prompt 2:** What datasets were used for training the model, and how were they selected?
3. **Prompt 3:** Discuss the performance metrics utilized to evaluate the model's success.
4. **Prompt 4:** Explain any biases that were identified in the training data.
5. **Prompt 5:** How was the model tested against previous benchmarks?
6. **Prompt 6:** Share any limitations that were found during the evaluation process.
7. **Prompt 7:** Describe the adjustments made to improve model performance post-evaluation.
8. **Prompt 8:** Outline plans for future updates and evaluations of the model.

## Deviation Mirror Structure
- **Introduction:** Briefly introduce the concept of the Deviation Mirror and its relevance to the protocol.
- **Deviations Observed:** List the deviations noted during testing and evaluation with respective dates, descriptions, and impact analysis.
- **Remedial Actions Taken:** Document actions taken to address each deviation, emphasizing corrective measures.
- **Follow-up Evaluations:** Outline follow-up assessments to ensure corrections have been effective and how future deviations will be mitigated.
